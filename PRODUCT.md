# Product

## Register
product

## What it is
Print Studio is a local web app for one person's printer and scanner. It sits beside the print-mcp server: everything the AI can do through MCP, a person can do here, and the other way round. One paper-first workspace holds imported files, scans and typed text; printing is the last step.

## Users
Thai-speaking home and small-office users on Windows with an inkjet all-in-one (Brother DCP-T520W in this case). They print forms, scan IDs and documents, centre them on the sheet, add a line of text, and print. The page stays open on a second screen all day. Many are not designers: labels must say what a control does.

## Brand
Practical, calm, exact. A proofing desk, not a toy: paper under good light, a few real instruments around it. Colour is information, not decoration.

## Visual world
- Warm neutral bench, white sheet, ink-blue for the person, vermilion for the AI (its cursor and label only).
- IBM Plex Sans Thai for UI, IBM Plex Mono for measurements, shortcuts and numbers.
- Solid surfaces, hairlines, small radii, soft offset shadows only on things that float.

## Anti-references
Canva-style purple gradients, glass panels, tinted icon tiles in grids of identical cards, aurora backgrounds, emoji as icons, kicker labels above headings, bouncy springs.

## AI presence
When an MCP client calls a tool, a labelled cursor ("Claude") moves across the real controls, opens the real popups, fills the real fields and presses the real buttons, then the tool runs. The person can watch, take over with Esc, or ignore it.

## Assumptions (no interview possible; written from the brief)
Single user, localhost only, Thai first, light and dark follow the system, desktop first with a usable phone layout.
